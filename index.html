MoneyLogger is an offline-first personal finance app. The short version: your financial data stays on your device. We do not run any server, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

1. Who we are

MoneyLogger is a personal expense and income tracker for Android. There is no online service behind it — the app runs entirely on your phone.

2. What the app accesses, and why

Bank transaction SMS

With your permission, MoneyLogger reads bank and card transaction SMS on your device to automatically create entries in your expense/income ledger. A prominent in-app disclosure is shown before the permission is requested, and you can decline and enter transactions manually instead.

  • SMS are read and parsed on your device only.
  • SMS content is never uploaded to us or any third party. We have no server to send it to.
  • You can revoke SMS permission any time in Android Settings.

3. Where your data is stored

  • All transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, and settings are stored in a private database on your device.
  • We do not transmit this data to any server (we have none) and do not share it with advertisers, analytics providers, or any third party.
  • The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Any crash/diagnostic logs are kept locally on the device and are not sent anywhere.

4. Optional Google Drive backup

  • If enabled, a backup of your database is encrypted on your device and uploaded to your own Google Drive using your Google sign-in.
  • The backup lives in your Google account; we never receive or access it.
  • You can turn backup off and delete backups any time in the app.
  • If you do not enable it, no data ever leaves your device.

Your use of Google Drive is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with anyone.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • You can delete individual records, clear app data in the app, or uninstall to remove all local data.
  • Uninstalling does not remove Google Drive backups you created; delete those from your Drive if you wish.

7. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandboxing and your device security. You can add a PIN or biometric app lock inside MoneyLogger. Google Drive backups are encrypted.

8. Children

MoneyLogger is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the "Effective date" above reflects the latest version.
